# Explanation audit: guidelines

Each item shows one verse in bold, with the verse before and after it in grey for context, and the
explanation we generated for it with Gemini 2.0 Flash. These explanations are what our "AI
explanation" index contains, and we want to know how often they are faithful.

Faithful to the verse?

- 2: the explanation says what the verse means. The wording can differ, but the sense is right.
- 1: partly. The gist is right, but there is a small error, an important image is left out, or the
  paraphrase is so generic it would fit many verses.
- 0: wrong. It misreads the verse (wrong subject, wrong sense, or the opposite meaning).

Adds content not in the verse? Does the explanation state things the verse does not say (names,
events, context, a moral) beyond what a reader would normally infer? "Minor" means a small
embellishment. "Substantive" means a claim that could mislead a reader about the verse.

Judge the verse as it is. Some verses are obscure. If you cannot tell what a verse means, accept the
explanation only if it is plausible, and add a note. There are 50 items; the whole task takes about
45 minutes.
